# About Me

My name is Bob Debus. I graduated in 2020 with a Master of Science in Mechanical Engineering from KU Leuven – Group T.
After graduating, I decided to broaden my skills with a postgraduate program at KU Leuven. During this year, I worked on hands-on projects at CORE, a student company focused on making a socially responsible contribution to smarter and more efficient energy use.
I then started my professional career at DUSS, a company focused on sustainability in building construction, where I worked as a sustainability consultant for a year.
After that first step into the professional world, I wanted to move closer to technology and data. I joined Agiliz, a data consultancy company, as a trainee, where I strengthened my SQL and data modelling skills and gained hands-on experience with tools such as Alteryx, MicroStrategy and Power BI.
After a few months, I started my first project at Atlas Copco in Wilrijk.

## Atlas Copco

At Atlas Copco, I was responsible for monthly financial and operational reporting across distribution centers worldwide. I worked extensively with MicroStrategy and managed thousands of reports used across the organization.
One of my main responsibilities was leading the migration from MicroStrategy to Power BI. As project manager, I was responsible for setting up the migration approach, structuring the different steps, coordinating stakeholders and keeping track of deadlines.
Alongside the BI migration, I also worked on the underlying data processes. I migrated SQL-based ETL processes from MicroStrategy to Databricks and built a central data product that calculated and standardized KPIs.
This project taught me an important lesson: building a reliable data foundation is just as important as building the dashboard on top of it. Having a single source of truth significantly improved the reliability and consistency of reporting across the organization.

## Telenet

Since June 2026, I have been working at Telenet as an Analytics Engineer.
In my current role, I act as a bridge between business stakeholders and data engineers. I gather requirements from the business, translate them into technical specifications and design clear data flows that can be implemented by the data engineering team.
The data comes from various CRM applications and is brought together into a coherent data product with clearly defined inputs, transformations and outputs. My work is primarily focused on customer data and involves extensive use of SQL and Snowflake.
This role has further developed my skills in stakeholder management, data modelling, SQL and data product design. More importantly, it has strengthened my interest in understanding not only how data is analysed, but also how robust and scalable data products are built.
