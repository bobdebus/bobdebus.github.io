My name is Bob Debus. I currently work at **Agiliz**, a data consultancy company, where I work on data and analytics projects for different clients.

I graduated in 2020 with a Master of Science in Mechanical Engineering from KU Leuven – Group T. After graduating, I broadened my skills through a postgraduate program at KU Leuven, where I worked on hands-on projects focused on smarter and more efficient energy use.

My career started in sustainability consulting, but I soon realized that I was more interested in technology, data and problem-solving. I joined **Agiliz** as a trainee, where I developed my skills in SQL and data modelling and gained experience with tools such as Alteryx, MicroStrategy and Power BI.

Through Agiliz, I have since worked on data and BI projects for companies including **Atlas Copco** and **Telenet**. These experiences have given me a broad understanding of how businesses work with data, from translating business questions into data requirements to building reports, data products and data flows.

What I enjoy most is the combination of **technology, problem-solving and data**. I’m particularly interested in understanding what happens behind the dashboards: how data is collected, transformed, modelled and turned into reliable products that people can actually use.

## Where I’m going

I’m currently focusing on growing from a BI and Analytics background towards **Data and AI Engineering**.

I’m using this website to document that journey — the technologies I’m learning, the projects I’m building and the things I discover along the way.

Long term, I would love to combine my technical interests with my passion for **sports or sustainability**. These two topics are two fields that have always interested me, and I’d like to explore how data, technology and AI can create new opportunities in either of them.

This website is a place to learn, build and share that journey.
